# Human Gates

The router must not bypass these gates.

## Always Manual

- final public theme approval;
- Research Synthesis direction approval;
- draft voice and language-fit approval;
- final source package lock;
- final image or cover approval;
- Publishing Prep approval;
- publication, posting, sending, emailing, outreach, or contacting anyone;
- changes to external systems;
- private transcript promotion into tracked canon;
- unrelated archive ingestion.

## Routing Rule

If the next action depends on one of these gates, classify as `manual_gate` or `blocked`.

Repeat, continue, resume, go on, or heartbeat language is not approval to skip a gate.

## Allowed Preparation

The router may recommend preparing an artifact for Vitor to review.

The router may not approve the artifact, act externally, or claim publication readiness on Vitor's behalf.
