# Echo Ingest Research Workflow

Use this skill to turn sources into research notes that keep evidence, interpretation, and open questions separate.

## Workflow

1. Ensure source intake is complete or mark the missing extraction blocker.
2. Build an evidence table:
   - claim;
   - supporting source;
   - confidence;
   - caveat;
   - useful quote only when brief and allowed.
3. Separate:
   - observed facts;
   - author claims;
   - your synthesis;
   - implications for Vitor or TheHive;
   - unanswered questions.
4. Use selected patterns only when they improve the research objective.
5. Route outputs through `$echo-ingest-result-review` before final note promotion.

## Good Research Notes

Good notes are source-linked, compact, and reusable. They do not bury the user in transcript residue or treat a single source as settled truth.

## Output

Return:

- research question;
- source coverage;
- evidence table;
- synthesis;
- uncertainty;
- recommended next read or decision.
